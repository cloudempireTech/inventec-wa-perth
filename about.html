Engineering Heritage & Precision

Three Decades of Western Australian Engineering

From custom toolmaking to heavy industrial machining and structural fabrication in Maddington.

Our History

Built on Precision Toolmaking Foundations

Inventec WA Pty Ltd originated as Quality Moulds and Tools, establishing its reputation by manufacturing high-tolerance injection moulds, stamping dies, and intricate tooling where deviations of a single micron were unacceptable.

Over the decades, we expanded our Maddington facility at 16 Church Road into a comprehensive multi-bay engineering complex. Today, we combine high-speed multi-axis CNC machining centres, heavy horizontal boring mills, wire EDM, and certified structural metal fabrication to service tier-1 mining operators, subsea oil & gas contractors, and heavy industrial plants.

Zero Defect Commitment

Every component is verified against strict Australian and international engineering standards. We provide complete mill certificates, ultrasonic test reports, and coordinate measuring machine inspection documentation.

Facility Infrastructure

Fully Equipped Machine Shop & Heavy Bay

Heavy overhead cranes, climate-controlled inspection rooms, and full CAD/CAM toolpathing.

Advanced CAD/CAM Integration

Direct ingestion of native SolidWorks, STEP, IGES, DXF, and CATIA models. Automated toolpath simulation eliminates tool collisions and ensures exact geometric adherence.

Comprehensive Toolroom Bay

Surface grinding up to large plate capacities, tool & cutter sharpeners, cylindrical OD/ID grinding, and wire EDM capable of cutting hardened alloys up to 65 HRC.

24/7 Mining Shift Mobilization

Our workshop can run 24-hour split shifts during planned mine shutdowns, maritime dry docks, or emergency Pilbara breakdown overhauls.
