Quality Assurance

Coordinate Measuring & Material Traceability

Formerly known as Quality Moulds and Tools, Inventec WA has built its Western Australian reputation on zero-defect manufacturing. We maintain rigorous quality control checkpoints at every step:

  • CMM Dimensional Verification: Climate-controlled coordinate measuring machine scanning ensures exact conformity to your CAD drawing.
  • Full Mill Test Certificates: 100% material traceability from certified Australian and international steel mills.
  • Surface Treatments & Hardening: Vacuum heat treatment, case hardening, hard chrome plating, metal spraying, and powder coating.
  • Integrated CAD/CAM Engineering: Direct translation of STEP, IGES, DXF, DWG, and SolidWorks model files.
